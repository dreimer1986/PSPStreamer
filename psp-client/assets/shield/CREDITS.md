The original user-supplied GLB is retained here. PSP adaptation by the
PSPStreamer project: skin baked in its rest pose, centered and oriented across
the tunnel; 42 triangles retained. Embedded base-color texture reduced to
128×128 and red emission recolored neon green, retaining the honeycomb pattern.
Rendered with a subtle pulsing additive glow. No runtime skeletal animation.

Rebuild using `python3 tools/import_cave_shield.py
psp-client/assets/shield/astro_shield__rigged.glb psp-client/cave_shield_data.h`
(NumPy and Pillow required). Asset licensing is independent of the code license.
